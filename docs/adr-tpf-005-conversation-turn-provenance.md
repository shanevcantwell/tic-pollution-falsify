**Date**: 2026-07-03

`../HANDOFF-2026-07-03-provenance-audit-and-corpus-walls.md` §1); drafted by the

**Related**: ADR-TPF-001/002/003 (reclassified by this record), PR #1 (held)

---

## Context

A provenance audit found the existing ADR family's justificatory apparatus —
the activation-steering/off-manifold rejections in ADR-TPF-001 §Alternatives,
and derivative material — was authored inside the drafting model's private
thinking traces (`../notes/metadiscussion-of-h0-generation.md` L49, L55), never
in a conversation turn the operator participated in. The entailment review
(`../reviews/2026-07-03-h0-family-review.md`) validated circularly: its
"genesis material" includes the drafting monologue itself. The category the
pipeline never tracked: **conversation turn vs. thinking trace**. Operator:
"The methodology here got engineered below the conversation."

## Decision

1. A TPF decision record carries decision authority **only** from operator
   conversation turns, cited by file/date in the record.
2. Thinking-trace and model-drafted material is admissible as **proposal
   only** — filed as `proposed` with its machine provenance named, never
   presented as a settled decision.
3. ADR-TPF-001, -002, -003 and `../loose-ends.md` are reclassified
   **model-drafted, ratification pending** until per-record operator verdicts
   (adopt / demote / drop). PR #1 stays held meanwhile.
4. Nothing is deleted: the record shows what was machine-drafted and when the
   operator ruled — demote-not-delete.

## Rationale

The rejected alternative is the status quo — trusting the drafting pipeline's
output as decisions. This session demonstrated its failure mode end-to-end:
manufactured alternatives, circular review, third-derivative repairs (PR #1).
The mechanism is documented in the kudzu/manufactured-rigor records
(thought-vault-integration behavioral-dynamics 0005; ADR-FORGE-0001): the
apparatus was fitted to a mind-type, not grounded in the work.
