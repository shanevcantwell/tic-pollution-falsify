The correction first: in a decoder-only transformer with causal masking, prior tokens' contextual representations are not rebuilt when a new token arrives. Each token's representation at every layer is computed attending only leftward, so once "orthogonal" is emitted at position t, its keys and values at every layer are fixed — that's exactly what the KV cache exploits.
